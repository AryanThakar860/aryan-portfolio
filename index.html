DIMENSION 05 // FAMILY DYNASTY

A Legacy Written in Crimson & Gold

In the mist-kissed plateau of Pampore, where the Himalayas cast eternal watch over fields of purple crocus, our family has guarded the sacred saffron bulb for over four decades.

What began in 1982 with my grandfather’s heirloom soil plot has blossomed into an internationally celebrated standard of purity. Every autumn at 4:30 AM before sunrise strikes the valley, three generations of our family kneel in the dew to hand-gather each blossom before the petals open to sunlight.

The three crimson stigmas of each flower are excised with thumb and forefinger within 6 hours of picking, air-dried over traditional silk screens, and sealed hermetically to lock in the volatile safranal essences.
